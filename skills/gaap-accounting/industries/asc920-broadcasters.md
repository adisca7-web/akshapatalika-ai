# ASC 920 — Entertainment: Broadcasters

> Industry skill distilled from *Wiley GAAP 2020*, ch. 64 (Specialized Industry GAAP).
> Rules and structure synthesised locally; no source text reproduced.

**Applies to** enterprises (or affiliated groups) that transmit radio or television programme material. (ASC 920-10-05-1, 920-10-20)

---

## Core model

A programme licence is **a purchase of rights, not a rental**. The broadcaster pays the fee whether or not it ever televises the programme, and unexercised rights simply revert to the licensor with no refund. So the economics are those of an acquired asset with deferred payment, and ASC 920 records it that way: gross up an asset and a liability at the start of the licence period, then amortise the asset by *use* and unwind the liability by *payment*.

Two independent decisions follow: how to measure the liability (gross or present value), and how to amortise the asset (by telecast, by series, or over the licence period).

---

## Subskills

### 1. Recognise the licence
Record the asset and liability when the licence period begins **and** all three conditions are met (ASC 920-350-25-2):
1. The cost of each programme is known or reasonably determinable.
2. The programme material has been accepted per the agreement.
3. The broadcaster has access to the programme for the first telecast — unless an agreement with another licensee prevents that telecast.

All three, not any. A signed contract alone does not trigger recognition.

### 2. Measure the liability
Two permitted bases (ASC 920-405-30-1):

| Basis | Liability | Interest |
|---|---|---|
| **Gross** | Face amount of the obligation | None accrued; amortisation is the only expense |
| **Fair value** | Present value of the payments | Difference between gross and net is accounted for as interest over the payment term |

Under either basis, allocate the capitalised cost across programmes in the package by **each programme's relative value to the broadcaster** — typically expected advertising revenue, not equal splits.

### 3. Present it correctly
- The programme rights asset is presented **separately from films** (ASC 920-405-45-1).
- Split the asset **current / noncurrent by expected time of programme usage**.
- Split the liability **by payment maturity** — a different cut. The two do not mirror each other, and assuming they do is a common presentation error.
- Network affiliation agreements are reported as **intangible assets** (ASC 920-350-45-2).

### 4. Amortise by usage pattern
Capitalised licence cost is amortised as the rights are used, generally on estimated number of telecasts (ASC 920-350-35-1).

| Situation | Method |
|---|---|
| Licence grants a specified number of showings | Amortise per telecast, using each showing's share of expected revenue |
| Licence grants **unlimited** showings (e.g. cartoons) | Amortise over the **licence period** — telecast count is indeterminable |
| Feature programmes | Programme-by-programme, unless package amortisation gives approximately the same result |
| Syndicated series, first showing more valuable than reruns | Amortise **as a series, accelerated** |
| Syndicated series, each showing equally valuable | Amortise **as a series, straight-line** |

### 5. Carry at the lower of cost or fair value
Report capitalised programme rights at the lower of unamortised cost or fair value (ASC 920-350-30-3). The comparison may be made on a **package, series, programme-by-programme, or daypart** basis — the entity picks the unit and applies it consistently.

Where usefulness is expected to diminish, write unamortised cost down to fair value through income. That **establishes a new cost basis and may not be restored** in a later period (ASC 920-350-35-3).

### 6. Account for network affiliation changes
On termination with immediate replacement, or an agreement to replace (ASC 920-350-40-1):
- **Loss** = unamortised cost of the previous affiliation **less fair value of the new affiliation**.
- **No gain** is recognised if the new affiliation's fair value exceeds unamortised cost.
- If the terminated affiliation is **not** replaced, charge the whole unamortised cost to expense.

Programme costs are generally lower for an affiliate than an independent station, because the affiliate incurs no programme cost for network programming and is compensated under a formula reflecting advertising sold on a network basis.

## Decision rules

| If | Then | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Licence signed but first-telecast access not yet available | Do not recognise the asset or liability yet | 920-350-25-2 |
| Package covers several programmes | Allocate cost by relative value to the broadcaster | 920-405-30-1 |
| Liability measured at present value | Accrue the gross-minus-net difference as interest | 920-405-30-1 |
| Liability measured gross | Accrue no interest | 920-405-30-1 |
| Unlimited showings permitted | Amortise over the licence period, not per telecast | 920-350-35-1 |
| First run worth more than reruns | Accelerated amortisation of the series | 920-350-35-2 |
| Unamortised cost exceeds fair value and usefulness is diminishing | Write down; new cost basis | 920-350-35-3 |
| Written-down programme later performs well | No restoration | 920-350-35-3 |
| Affiliation terminated and replaced, new FV > unamortised cost | Recognise **no gain** | 920-350-40-1 |
| Affiliation terminated and not replaced | Expense the unamortised cost in full | 920-350-40-1 |

## Anti-patterns

- **Treating a programme licence as an operating expense as paid.** It is a rights purchase; capitalise at the start of the licence period.
- **Recognising on contract signature.** All three ASC 920-350-25-2 conditions, including first-telecast access, must be met.
- **Splitting asset and liability on the same current/noncurrent boundary.** The asset follows usage timing; the liability follows payment maturity.
- **Accruing interest under the gross method.** Interest arises only when the liability is measured at fair value.
- **Allocating package cost evenly across programmes.** Allocation is by relative value to the broadcaster.
- **Amortising an unlimited-showings cartoon licence per telecast.** Use the licence period.
- **Restoring a programme write-down** after a ratings recovery. Prohibited.
- **Booking a gain on a favourable affiliation swap.** Gains are never recognised on affiliation replacement.

## Key terms

- **Broadcaster** — an enterprise or affiliated group transmitting radio or television programme material.
- **Daypart** — an aggregation of programmes broadcast in a particular time slot (daytime, evening, late night) or of a similar type (sports, news, children's). Broadcasters sell audience access to advertisers on this basis, which is why it is a permitted unit for the lower-of-cost-or-fair-value test.
- **Licence agreement** — covers a package of programmes and grants a station, network, pay TV, or cable system the right to broadcast a specified or unlimited number of showings over a maximum licence period for a specified fee.
- **Network affiliation agreement** — compensates a station for carrying network programming under a formula reflecting advertising sold on a network basis.
