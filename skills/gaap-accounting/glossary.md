# Glossary

Terms with the topic that defines them. Industry terms link to the industry file; core terms give the chapter.

## A–C

**Accrued account balance** — deposits net of withdrawals, plus amounts credited, less fees, plus additional interest and other adjustments. (944 insurance)
**Accretable yield** — cash flows expected to be collected in excess of the initial investment on acquired loans. (942)
**Accumulated plan benefits** — future benefit payments attributable to service rendered to the benefit information date. (960)
**Agent** *(NFP)* — a recipient that uses or transfers assets for a donor-specified beneficiary, or that a beneficiary can compel to distribute. (958)
**Amenities** — features enhancing a project's attractiveness or perceived value: golf courses, clubhouses, pools. (970)
**Area franchise** — rights within a geographic area permitting multiple outlets, with outlet decisions made largely by the franchisee. (952)
**Backplant** — a title plant antedating the period covered by the existing plant. (950)
**Base jackpot** — the fixed minimum slot payout for a specific combination; **not a liability while avoidable**. (924)
**Cable television plant** — head-end, cable and amplifiers, drops, converters and descramblers. (922)
**Collections** — art or historical treasures that are held for public exhibition rather than gain, protected and preserved, **and** subject to a policy requiring sale proceeds to buy other collection items. All three. (958)
**Common costs** — costs relating to two or more units within a project. (970)
**Contract asset / contract liability** — the net position by contract depending on whether performance leads or lags billing. (ch38)
**Continuing franchise fee** — consideration for continuing rights and services during the agreement term. (952)
**Cost-plus-fixed-fee contract** — reimbursement of costs plus a fixed fee. (912)
**Cross-collateralized** — distribution rights to multiple films, territories, or markets whose exploitation results are aggregated. (926)
**Current (normal) servicing fee rate** — the rate common in comparable servicing agreements; the benchmark for the sale-price adjustment. (948)

## D–F

**Daypart** — programmes aggregated by time of day or type; a permitted unit for the broadcast lower-of-cost test. (920)
**Designated net assets** — net assets without donor restrictions subject to **self-imposed** board limits. (958)
**Detail program design** — the design taking functions, features, and technical requirements to their most detailed logical form, enabling coding. (985)
**Direct selling costs** — commissions, non-commission acquisition compensation, local acquisition advertising, and new-subscriber document processing; **excludes** supervisory, administrative, and indirect costs. (922)
**Disposal credits** — amounts deducted from a termination claim because the contractor retained or sold termination inventory. (912)
**Donor-imposed condition** — a barrier plus a right of return. **Delays recognition.** (958)
**Donor-imposed restriction** — a stipulation narrowing use. **Affects classification only, not timing.** (958)
**Economic interest** *(NFP)* — the other entity holds resources that must be used for the reporting entity's purposes, or the reporting entity is responsible for its liabilities. (958)
**Endowment fund** — an established fund providing income for an NFP's maintenance; donor-restricted or board-designated. (958)
**Energising the system** — providing the cable signal for the first time. (922)
**Equalization** — crediting/charging per-share undistributed income on share sales and repurchases. (946)
**Exploitation costs** — direct costs of a film's distribution: marketing, advertising, publicity, promotion. (926)
**Film costs** — direct negative costs, production overhead, and capitalised production-period interest. (926)
**Financially interrelated** *(NFP)* — one entity can influence the other's operating and financial decisions **and** has an ongoing economic interest in its net assets. (958)
**Fixed-odds contract** — a wager whose odds are known when placed; accounted for under **ASC 606**, not ASC 815. (924)
**Front-end load** — a sales charge payable when mutual fund shares are purchased. (946)
**Fully benefit-responsive** — an investment contract meeting five conditions permitting participant transactions at contract value. (946, 960)

## G–N

**Gap commitment** — interim financing bridging the floor loan and the maximum permanent loan. (948)
**Incidental operations** — revenue activities during holding or development that **reduce development cost**; excess revenue reduces capitalised cost. (970, 978)
**Incremental costs of incidental operations** — those that would not be incurred but for the incidental operations; interest, taxes, insurance, and security are **not** incremental. (970)
**Incremental direct costs** — costs resulting directly from and essential to a transaction that would not otherwise have been incurred. (942, 944)
**Individual-film-forecast method** — amortise cost by current revenue over remaining ultimate revenue at the start of the year. (926)
**Internal replacement** — a modification of an insurance product's benefits, features, rights, or coverages. (944)
**Internal reserve method** — a GNMA issuer method requiring a custodial deposit of one month's interest. (948)
**Interval** — the specific period, usually a week, a time-share unit is available to a customer. (978)
**Joint activity** — an activity combining fund raising with a program or management function. Presumed **all** fund raising unless purpose, audience, and content are all met. (958)
**Liquidation** — converting assets to cash and settling obligations in anticipation of ceasing activities; **acquisition or merger with intent to continue is not liquidation**. (960)
**Market** *(film)* — a distribution channel in a territory: theatrical, home video, pay TV, free TV, merchandise licensing. (926)
**Minimum guarantee** — an amount paid in advance by a licensee for distribution rights. (928)
**Natural expense classification** — grouping by the kind of economic benefit: salaries, benefits, rent, utilities, depreciation. (958)
**Net assets** *(NFP)* — assets less liabilities, in exactly two classes: **with** and **without donor restrictions**. (958)
**Net assets** *(plans)* — the residual after liabilities, which **exclude** accumulated plan benefits or benefit obligations. (960)
**Net asset value per share** — net assets per share of capital stock, excluding the effect of assuming conversion of convertibles. (946)
**Net-spread method** — treats acquiring a savings and loan as acquiring a **leveraged whole**. (942)
**Nonaccretable difference** — contractual cash flows in excess of expected cash flows. (942)
**No-cost settlement** — a termination in which the contractor makes no claim and retains inventory at its own risk; **no sale, no profit**. (912)

## O–S

**Overall deal** — compensation for exclusive or preferential use of a creative individual's services. (926)
**Participation costs** — contingent compensation to talent and rights licensors via participations and residuals. (926)
**Permanent difference** — a book/tax difference that never reverses; moves the effective rate but creates **no** deferred tax. (ch47)
**Permanent investor** — an entity investing in mortgage loans for its own account. (948)
**Phase** — a contractually or physically distinguishable portion of a real estate or time-sharing project. (970, 978)
**PIK bond** — pays interest in further debt instruments ("baby" bonds) maturing with the parent. (946)
**Preacquisition costs** — costs incurred for the express purpose of, but before, obtaining a property. (970)
**Prematurity period** — the window in which a cable system is partly built and partly in service; presumed **≤ 2 years** and effectively irrevocable. (922)
**Product enhancement** — improvements extending life or significantly improving marketability; normally needs its own product design. (985)
**Record master** — the master tape from an artist's performance. (928)
**Relative fair value before construction** — each parcel's fair value relative to others, **excluding** value added by development. (970)
**Set for production** — funding committed, active pre-production begun, principal photography expected within **6 months**. (926)
**Spending rate** — the portion of total investment return used for current fiscal needs, usually a percentage of a moving average market value. (958)
**Split-interest agreement** — a transfer whose ownership benefits are split among two or more beneficiaries. (958)
**Step bond** — pays no interest for a period, then escalating stipulated rates. (946)
**Surplus notes** — insurer securities accounted for as **debt**, with interest accrued regardless of regulatory payment approval. (944)

## T–Z

**Technological feasibility** — the point at which planning, design, coding, and testing establish the product can meet its design specifications; the switch from R&D expense to capitalisation. (985)
**Temporary difference** — a book/tax difference that **will reverse**, creating deferred tax. (ch47)
**Tenancy-for-years** — a qualified right of possession for a term, reverting afterwards. (978)
**Territory** — the geographic area of film exploitation, usually a country. (926)
**Title plant** — a historical record of all matters affecting title to land in an area; capitalised and **never amortised** absent impairment. (950)
**Trustee** *(NFP)* — holds and manages assets for a **specified** beneficiary; NFPs holding for a community are **not** trustees in this sense. (958)
**Ultimate revenue** — estimated total revenue from a film across all markets over the permitted horizon; **gross and undiscounted**. (926)
**Undivided interest** — the right to joint use of a title plant (950); or a tenant-in-common time-share interest (978).
**Variance power** — the unilateral power to redirect transferred assets to a different beneficiary; shifts contribution revenue to the recipient. **Must be granted by the transferring instrument.** (958)
**Working model** — an operative version in the final language, performing all major functions, ready for beta testing. (985)
**Wrapper contract** — transfers the risk of a crediting rate falling below zero to a third party. (946, 960)
